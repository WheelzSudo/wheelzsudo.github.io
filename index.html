Why Unit Economics & Pricing Accuracy Drive Business Growth

Whether operating as an independent freelancer, running a digital agency, launching a SaaS platform, or scaling an e-commerce store, inaccurate pricing models erode profitability. Operating on thin margins without factoring in overhead, taxes, payment processing fees, or churn leads to phantom revenue.

How Our Business Calculators Help You Scale

  • Service Rate Optimization: Our service calculators (Freelance, Agency, Personal Trainer, and Trade Contractor) calculate target billing rates derived from net take-home salary goals, actual billable hours, and tax buffers.
  • Paid Advertising Efficiency: The Ad Spend & ROAS calculator determines break-even return targets and maximum allowable target CPA before committing ad capital on Meta, Google, or TikTok.
  • Subscription & Recurring Revenue Modeling: The SaaS Churn & LTV calculator models gross margin contribution, customer lifespan, and churn impact to safeguard monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
  • Media & Creator Monetization: The Podcast Sponsorship and Course Launch calculators allow creators to price host-read ad spots using industry CPM standards and forecast launch conversion tiers.
